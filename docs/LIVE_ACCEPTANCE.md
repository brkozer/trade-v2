# Future live acceptance — not authorized

`LIVE_DISABLED` stays in force. Routine strategy configuration, a successful backtest,
testnet credentials or an operator-console button cannot remove it.

Before a separate governed live-canary proposal can be considered, require all of:

1. A frozen candidate with cost-aware accepted economics, complete trial history,
   untouched final validation and fresh prospective evidence at declared checkpoints.
2. Replay/real-time decision parity and simulated execution acceptance under stressed
   fees, spread, slippage, funding, partial fills and missed fills.
3. Authorized dedicated testnet lifecycle verification: entry/exit/protection, partial
   exits, ambiguous responses, reconnect, restart and exact exchange reconciliation.
4. Correct fee and settled funding attribution; explicit accounting completeness;
   account/equity reconciliation and no ambiguous ownership.
5. Canonical account identity and host authority, startup reconciliation, protective
   state, market/watchdog freshness, durable halts, tested backup/restore and recovery.
6. Clean required CI, source and artifact integrity, reviewed secrets boundaries,
   operational runbook, explicit capital limits and operator approval.

Future transport must use trusted built-in endpoints and a separately reviewed operator
authorization mechanism. Local locks cannot claim distributed protection. No single
statistical metric or infrastructure checklist substitutes for the combined evidence.
